# Akaun

Akaun is bookkeeping software for small teams and freelancers. You install it on your own
server, so your books stay with you. Each user signs in from a web browser.

Akaun keeps one list of records. Each record tells where money came from and where it went. AI
can read your receipts, bills and bank statements and prepare the records for you. Akaun also
makes quotations and invoices, matches your accounts to bank statements, and gives standard
reports. Many users can work in one book, and each user gets only the permissions that you give.

This guide is for the people who keep the books. It is also for the person who installs Akaun
and manages its users and settings.

Read [Getting started](./01-getting-started/install.md) first. Then read
[How Akaun works](./02-concepts/records.md) before you record a lot of data. Use the other
sections when you need them. The [Glossary](./10-help/glossary.md) gives the meaning of each term.
