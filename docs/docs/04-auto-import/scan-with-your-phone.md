# Scan with your phone

On a phone, **Auto Import** can scan a paper receipt with the camera. Akaun finds the edges of the
page, makes the image flat and clear, and adds it to the queue as a PDF.

The **Scan** button shows only on a narrow screen, such as a phone. On a computer, take a photo
and upload it. See [Import receipts and documents](./import-receipts.md).

## Scan a receipt

1. Open **Auto Import** on your phone.
2. In **Read as**, choose how Akaun reads the scan.
3. Put the receipt on a flat surface with good light.
4. Tap **Scan** at the bottom right of the screen.
5. Take a photo of the receipt with the camera, and accept it.
6. Wait until the text "Finding document…" goes away. The title of the screen is **Review scan**.
7. Check the image. If you must change it, use the tools in the next section.
8. Tap **Save**.

## Correct the image

The bar at the bottom of **Review scan** has these tools:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Retake** | Opens the camera again for a new photo. |
| **Adjust** | Shows the four corners of the page. Drag each corner to the edge of the page. Tap **Apply** to keep the change, **Reset** to start again, or **Cancel**. |
| **Rotate** | Turns the image by a quarter turn, clockwise. |
| **Filter** | Shows three filters: **Colors**, **Grayscale** and **B&W**. Tap one to use it. **B&W** is the default and usually gives the clearest text. |
| **Save** | Makes the PDF and adds it to the queue. |

To stop without a scan, tap **Cancel** at the top left.

## Result

Akaun shows "Preparing document...", and then closes the scanner. The scan shows in the
**Processing queue** as a PDF with a name such as `scan-1760000000000.pdf`. Review it as usual.
See [Review and confirm](./review-and-confirm.md).

## Notes and limits

- One scan makes a PDF with one page. For a document with many pages, use a scanner app that makes
  one PDF. Then upload the PDF.
- The scanner loads its tools from the server the first time. If the message "Could not load the
  scanner" shows, check the connection and try again.
- The scan is a picture of the page. Akaun reads its text with text recognition (OCR), so a sharp
  photo gives a better result.
